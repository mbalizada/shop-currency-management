Quick Guide

1. Record the amounts collected from each shop in Weekly Collection.

2. When money reaches an exchange-center account, record it as Receive.

3. When money is sent out, record it as Send.

Collection records and account transactions are separate records. Recording a collection does not automatically increase an account balance.
